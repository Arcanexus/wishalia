Wishalia

Self-hosted wishlists that keep the surprise.

Get started Source code

Why Wishalia

  • Nothing spoiled

    The owner of a list never sees who gave what, until they lift the secret after the event. Enforced on the server, not hidden in the page.

  • Gifts together

    Several people chip in for one gift, across birthdays and Christmases, with a clear progress bar.

  • Lists and events

    Durable lists, recurring events, book series with the next volume, recurring wishes. Each item shows its picture, taken from the shop's page or your own photo, framed as you like.

  • For every age

    Large touch targets, an item added in under five seconds from any device, and no password to remember: a fingerprint, a face or a link by email is enough. It installs on the phone like an app, and « Share » from any shop adds a wish.

  • Private or public

    Each list is private, seen only by the people you send a link to, or public: found from the home page by your first name, and open to anyone who wants to take part.

  • Fast and light

    Little JavaScript, built to stay quick on a modest phone.

  • Yours

    Runs on your own Kubernetes cluster with one Helm chart, with Prometheus metrics that never give a gift away, and a REST API with personal tokens for your scripts and home automation. Your family's data stays at home.

Install

helm install wishalia oci://ghcr.io/arcanexus/charts/wishalia --version 0.0.1 \
  --set hosts.public=wishalia.example.com \
  --set hosts.app=app.wishalia.example.com \
  --set ingress.enabled=true

PostgreSQL comes with the chart, or bring your own with postgresql.enabled=false. On NFS, add volumePermissions.enabled=true.
