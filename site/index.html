wishlist

Self-hosted wishlists that keep the surprise.

Get started Source code

Why wishlist

  • Nothing spoiled

    The owner of a list never sees who gave what, until they lift the secret after the event. Enforced on the server, not hidden in the page.

  • Gifts together

    Several people chip in for one gift, across birthdays and Christmases, with a clear progress bar.

  • Lists and events

    Durable lists, recurring events, book series with the next volume, recurring wishes.

  • For every age

    Large touch targets, no password to remember, an item added in under five seconds from any device.

  • Fast and light

    Little JavaScript, built to stay quick on a modest phone.

  • Yours

    Runs on your own Kubernetes cluster with one Helm chart. Your family's data stays at home.

Install

helm install wishlist oci://ghcr.io/arcanexus/charts/wishlist \
  --set hosts.public=wishlist.example.com \
  --set hosts.app=app.wishlist.example.com \
  --set ingress.enabled=true
